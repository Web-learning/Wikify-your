Pitching to event organisers

Approach the organisers as early as possible.

Explain that Wikimedia activities can add value to the conference by making its knowledge and participants more discoverable and accessible. Keep the proposal practical and make it clear what you are asking from the organisers.

You might ask organisers to:

Allow Wikimedia contributors to attend or participate.
Include a Wikimedia activity in the programme.
Share the event programme and speaker information.
Introduce you to speakers and attendees.
Provide permission or guidance for photography.
Help identify useful sources and documentation.
Share information about Wikimedia activities with attendees.

Avoid presenting Wikimedia as simply a way of promoting the conference. The focus should be on sharing knowledge and improving freely available information.
