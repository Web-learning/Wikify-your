# Communications

Coordinate communications with the organisers before the event.

Prepare short, accessible explanations of what you are doing and why. These can be adapted for email, newsletters, social media, event websites, or attendee communications.

Consider creating:

A Wikimedia project page for the conference.
A short introduction to Wikimedia activities.
Instructions for attendees who want to participate.
A list of relevant Wikimedia projects.
Links to useful editing and uploading guides.
A contact person for questions.

Make it easy for someone who has never edited Wikimedia projects before to understand how they can participate.
