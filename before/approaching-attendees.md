# Approaching attendees

Don't wait until the conference begins to find contributors.

If the organisers are willing, introduce Wikimedia activities to attendees before the event. A short message can explain that contributors will be documenting and improving Wikimedia projects related to the conference.

You can invite attendees to:

Suggest articles that need improvement.
Provide freely usable photographs.
Check factual information about their work.
Add references to Wikipedia articles.
Improve Wikidata information about their organisations or research.
Participate in an editing session.
Connect Wikimedia contributors with other knowledgeable participants.

When approaching people, make the request specific. “Can you help with Wikimedia?” is less useful than “Would you be willing to check whether the information about your research project on Wikidata is accurate?”
