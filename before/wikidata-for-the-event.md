# Wikidata for the event

Consider creating or improving a Wikidata item for the conference itself.

Depending on the event, useful information may include:

Name of the conference.
Edition or year.
Start and end dates.
Location.
Organising organisation.
Official website.
Previous and subsequent editions.
Main topics or fields.
Speakers or participants.
Related events.

Wikidata can also help connect different editions of a recurring conference. Adding structured information makes the event easier to discover and allows information about it to be reused by other Wikimedia projects and external applications.

Check existing Wikidata items carefully before creating new ones. An event, organisation, or person may already have an item.
