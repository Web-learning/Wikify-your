# Wikidata for people

Conference programmes often provide a ready-made list of people who may be relevant to Wikidata.

Review the speakers and other prominent participants and check whether they already have Wikidata items.

For existing items, look for missing or outdated information such as:

Occupation.
Employer or affiliated organisation.
Field of work.
Country or place of residence.
Education.
Published works.
Official website.
Relevant identifiers.
Links to Wikipedia articles in different languages.

For people without Wikidata items, only create items where there is sufficient reliable information and where the person meets Wikidata's notability and sourcing expectations.

Be especially careful with personal information. Wikimedia projects are public resources, so avoid adding private or sensitive information simply because it is available somewhere online.
