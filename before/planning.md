#Planning to Wikify

Start by identifying what you want to achieve through the conference.

Possible activities include:

Identifying Wikipedia articles that could be created or improved.
Finding relevant people and organisations that should have Wikidata items.
Preparing a list of speakers, sessions, topics, and associated organisations.
Planning photography for Wikimedia Commons.
Recruiting experienced Wikimedians who can help during the event.
Inviting conference attendees to contribute.
Preparing documentation or editing activities around the conference programme.

It is useful to create a simple project page where participants can see what is happening, what needs to be done, and how they can help.

A good plan should also distinguish between activities that need to happen before, during, and after the event.
